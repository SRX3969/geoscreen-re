STEP 2 — Understand what a "site" means mathematically

This is your first actual coding task.

We decided:

User gives:
    center latitude
    center longitude
    area in km²

For v1, we'll represent the site as a square.

Suppose:

area = 1 km²

Then:

side = √1 = 1 km

If:

area = 4 km²

then:

side = √4 = 2 km

So:

side_km = sqrt(area_km2)

Your first function

Create src/site.py.

Write a function that receives:

latitude
longitude
area_km2

and calculates the side length of the square.

For example:

1 km² → 1 km × 1 km
4 km² → 2 km × 2 km
9 km² → 3 km × 3 km

STEP 4 — Learn one geographic conversion

Here's the only geographic concept you need right now.

Approximately:

1 degree latitude ≈ 111 km

Therefore:

1 km ≈ 1 / 111 degrees

So if your site is 1 km wide, we can convert its physical size into latitude/longitude offsets.

For longitude, the conversion changes depending on latitude, because longitude lines get closer together toward the poles.

We will handle that properly in the function.

You do not need to memorize this.
